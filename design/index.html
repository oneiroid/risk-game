DeF design system · v0.1

Meiji Steam

Japan, 1870s: samurai clans and the steam engine on the same field. Black lacquer and brass for surfaces, washi paper for anything you read up close, urushi red for attack and danger, indigo for sea. Two motifs repeat everywhere: the gear as the rim of a clan crest (mon), and the hanko seal for decisions.

01

Brand 紋章

The DeF crest is a brass gear rim around a lacquer disc. Minimum size 24 px; below 32 px drop the inner ring. The seal is for one-colour use: stamps, favicons on paper backgrounds, watermarks.

02

Colour 色

Dark lacquer is the default ground. Brass carries every primary action and focus state. Red is reserved for attack, danger and seals; it is never decorative.

03

Clans 一族

Map 地図

Real coastlines and borders (Natural Earth 1:50m), Miller projection, shaded relief from the same source blended over clan colours. Indigo sea with seigaiha waves, a lit continental shelf, brass coastline and a brass graticule like a navigator’s chart. Army tokens are clan-coloured coins with a brass rim. This is the live game board: drag, scroll or pinch to zoom.

08

Voice 言葉

  • UI text is functional. If removing a line loses nothing the player needs, remove it.
  • English first. Kanji are marks (clans, cards, seals), never the only label for an action.
  • Theme lives in names and materials, not in long flavour text: “Takeda unifies the realm”, not a paragraph.
  • Phases: Reinforce 増 · Attack 攻 · Fortify 固.
  • Red means loss or danger only. Brass means “do this”.
  • Numbers in mono, always: armies, dice, codes, card values.
